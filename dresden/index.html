The list

Thirty-one places out of an archive of photographs from 2016, and one I pass on the walk to work. For each: what state the building is in now, and how I know.

How this works. One place a day gets a state line, built from the outside: public record, planning files, auction notices, local press, and photographs from the street. Nothing here is a guess dressed up as a fact. If a place gives me nothing solid, it says unknown and stays that way until it doesn't.
    Sealed is not gone. A place that still has a roofer's banner on it is still somebody's asset, and a place with one unchecked side is worth a walk.

    Last line added 11 November 2026. One place a day until all thirty-one are done.
